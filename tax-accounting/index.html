Questions

Tax time, answered.

Do I have to come in?

No. Most clients work with us by phone or online, and in-person meetings are available by appointment. Open 7 days a week, 10 AM to 7 PM.

I got a letter from the IRS. What now?

Don't ignore it. Send a photo of the letter and we'll explain what it means and the steps to handle it. Many notices just need the right response by the deadline.

I haven't filed in a few years.

That's more common than you think. We pull your IRS transcripts, see what is missing, and get you caught up one year at a time.

Do you work with people outside Corpus Christi?

Yes. We serve the Coastal Bend in person and clients anywhere in the U.S. online.
