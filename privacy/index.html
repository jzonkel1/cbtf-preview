Privacy Policy

Coastal Bend Tax & Financial respects your privacy. This page explains what we collect through this website and how we use it.

What we collect

When you fill out a form, we receive the information you enter, such as your name, phone number, email and message. We use it only to respond to you and to provide the services you ask about.

What we don't do

We never sell or rent your information. When you ask us to connect you with a partner in our network, we share only what is needed for that introduction, and only with your request.

Security

Please don't send Social Security numbers, bank account numbers or tax documents through the website form. We'll give you a secure way to share documents when you become a client.
